**Full description**:

A bright, gentle bubble popping game for toddlers and preschoolers: tap or swipe to pop bubbles, balloons and stars.

**Pop with a tap or a swipe.** Bubbles, balloons and stars float up the screen. Tap one to pop it, or drag a finger across the screen and pop everything the line touches. Big targets and a forgiving touch area suit the smallest hands, and more than one finger can pop at once.

**Faces and sounds.** Many bubbles carry a friendly face with its own sound: frogs croak, ducks quack, cows moo, fish blub, owls hoot, and there is even a tractor that honks. A soft melody plays along, and every pop makes a note.

**Three scenes.** The game moves from a sunny sky to under the sea to a starry night, each with its own animals and decorations. Pop eight in a row and a rainbow Bubble Rush sends up extra bubbles. Every ten pops brings a cheer and a shower of confetti.

**A place for every child.** Each child picks their own face and name and keeps their own score, and the family leaderboard shows everyone's totals. A hold-to-finish home button means a stray tap never ends a round.
