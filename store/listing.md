A calm, bright popping game made for the smallest hands.

Tap or swipe to pop bubbles, balloons and stars as they float up through a sunny sky, under the sea, and across a starry night. Every bubble has a friendly animal face with its own sound: frogs croak, ducks quack, cows moo, owls hoot.

MADE FOR LITTLE KIDS

• Swipe a finger across the screen to pop everything it touches
• No wrong answers, no timers, no losing
• A hold-to-finish button so a stray tap never ends the fun

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Each child picks their own face and keeps their own score
• A family leaderboard shows who popped the most
• Cheers and confetti every ten pops

MADE FOR PARENTS
